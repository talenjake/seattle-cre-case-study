# Seattle Multifamily Acquisition Case Study

## Lake City Apartments | Seattle, Washington

Commercial real estate acquisition underwriting case study using public market data and clearly disclosed analyst assumptions.

### Read the Case Study

➡️ **[Open the full CRE investment case study](CASE_STUDY.md)**

### Key Results

- Purchase Price: **$1.50 million**
- Units: **8**
- In-Place Cap Rate: **6.08%**
- Year 1 NOI: **$91,200**
- Year 1 DSCR: **1.30x**
- Year 1 Cash-on-Cash Return: **3.4%**
- 5-Year Levered IRR: **10.1%**
- Equity Multiple: **1.57x**

### Project Scope

The analysis covers acquisition underwriting, NOI and cap-rate analysis, debt sizing, DSCR, five-year levered cash flow, exit valuation, IRR and equity multiple, sensitivity analysis, transaction comparables, and Seattle multifamily market research.
